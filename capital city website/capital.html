Sacramento, California

City Overview

Sacramento is the capital of California and is located in the Sacramento Valley. The city was incorporated in 1850 and has developed into an urban community and a center of state government.

Sacramento serves as the seat of California's state government and is located near the confluence of the Sacramento and American Rivers.

Income Compared With California

Sacramento's median household income was $87,321 for 2020–2024, measured in 2024 dollars. California's median household income for the same period was $99,122.

Based on these figures, Sacramento's median household income was lower than the California statewide median household income.
