San Diego, California

City Overview

San Diego is located in Southern California along the Pacific coast. The city was incorporated in 1850 and has developed into a major urban community in the region.

San Diego is part of the San Diego urban area and is one of California's largest cities by population.

Income Compared With California

San Diego's median household income was $108,077 for 2020–2024, measured in 2024 dollars. California's median household income for the same period was $99,122.

Based on these figures, San Diego's median household income was higher than the California statewide median household income.
