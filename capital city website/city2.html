San Francisco

A major urban city in the San Francisco Bay Area of Northern California.

San Francisco, California

City Overview

San Francisco is located on the northern California coast and forms part of the San Francisco Bay Area. The city was incorporated on April 15, 1850 and has developed into a densely populated urban community.

San Francisco is part of one of California's major metropolitan regions and is closely associated with the San Francisco Bay and surrounding Bay Area communities.

Income Compared With California

San Francisco's median household income was $140,970 for 2020–2024, measured in 2024 dollars. California's median household income for the same period was $99,122.

Based on these figures, San Francisco's median household income was higher than the California statewide median household income.
